##### Hardware
 * Augmented reality(AR)
   *With VR, designers can create virtual prototypes of their designs and see how they look at a model before they're even created in real life. This helps to speed up the design process and make it more efficient.
  * Sewing machines
    * When creating a piece of clothing, you only have one cloth that you cut into more pieces and use a sewing machine to put them back together but in a different order
      * For example, when making a T-shirt:
        * You have one piece of fabric
        * You measure and make marks with soap or chalk to know where to cut it
        * Then you carefully cut out the pieces of a future T-shirt
        * You use **Sewing machine** to connect all the pieces together
        * Now you have a T-shirt
