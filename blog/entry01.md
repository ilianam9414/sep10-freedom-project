## Why did I choose Architecture?
For this year-long project I chose architecture. The reason I chose architecture is because I find myself in a creative state every time I get to draw pieces and get to express myself. Ever since I can remember I have been creating a lot of paintings and had a lot of ideas of how I can draw something. Usually when I'm walking around and coming across the art supply stores  I don't think twice about going in, I love to look at all the supplies like paint, paint brushes, and canvases. I use many apps like Ibis paint and pro create to create a lot of my art as well I like it better than drawing on papers.  I love to make sketches of buildings and it's complicated designs as well as the structures of the buildings. 

The apps I used were, [Morpholio Trace](https://morpholioapps.com/trace/)
