# Entry 1: Architecture
##### 11.2.2026

## Why did I choose Architecture?
For this year-long project I chose architecture. The reason I chose architecture is because I find myself in a creative state every time I get to draw pieces and get to express myself. Ever since I can remember I have been creating a lot of paintings and had a lot of ideas of how I can draw something. Usually when I'm walking around and coming across the art supply stores  I don't think twice about going in, I love to look at all the supplies like paint, paint brushes, and canvases. I use many apps like Ibis paint and pro create to create a lot of my art as well I like it better than drawing on papers.  I love to make sketches of buildings and it's complicated designs as well as the structures of the buildings. I really like the way some buildings were created, for example I like Cologne Cathedral in Germany. I think this is a cool construction that has a strong foundation and it's really resilient. 

The two main links I used were, [Morpholio Trace](https://morpholioapps.com/trace/) and [Planner5](https://planner5d.com/use/architecture-design-software).
 The first link is a digital drawing and design app made mainly for architects. It allows creating sketches, floor plans, and detailed designs. And the second link is an online architecture and interior design platform that allows creating floor plans and 3D building designs.
