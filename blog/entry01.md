## Why did I choose Fashion?
### For this year-long project I chose Fashion. The reason I chose fashion is because I find myself in a creative state every time I get to sketch clothes pieces and get to express myself. Ever since I can remember I have been creating a lot of sketches of clothes and ideas of how I can style something and give it a second chance in life even if the clothes were ruined, it can became something new. Usually when i'm walking around and come across art shops I don't think twice about going in, I love to look at all the supplies like paint brushes, markers, canvases, and pencils. I use many apps like Ibis paint and pro create to create a lot of my art as well I like it better than drawing on papers.
