## Why did I choose Art?
### For this year-long project I choose Art. The reason I chose Art is because I find myself in a creative state every time I get to create art pieces and get to express myself. Ever since I can remember I have been creating art my parents have multiple paintings I've created over the years around my house and it makes me happy to know they appreciate my art. Usually when i'm walking around and come across art shops I don't think twice about going in, I love to look at all the supplies like paint brushes, markers, canvases, and pencils. I use many apps like Ibis paint and pro create to create a lot of my art as well I like it better than drawing on papers.
